The Woodman

The Woodman on Steam

The Woodman is an idle incremental game about chopping wood, managing resources, and automating transport. Put your resources to work to automate log hauling with gnome helpers, improve efficiency, and keep making progress as you idle in a colorful voxel world.

One very big tree

Start by chopping a towering tree and gathering wood. Manage the resources you collect to improve your operation, building up from hands-on work to steady idle progress.

Automate the hauling

Use resource management to automate transport with gnome helpers. Let them handle the log hauling while you focus on your next upgrade and make your operation more efficient.

Make time for a break

Visit the hot spring for an upgrade, cast a fishing line by the water, or settle down beside the fire after a day’s work.
