Description

The Woodman is an idle incremental game about chopping wood, managing resources, and automating transport. Put your resources to work to automate log hauling with gnome helpers, improve efficiency, and keep making progress as you idle in a colorful voxel world.

Features

  • Idle incremental progression built around woodcutting and upgrades
  • Resource management to automate log transport with gnome helpers
  • Improving efficiency as your operation grows
  • A village, forest, and underground mine to discover
  • Hot spring upgrades, fishing, and quiet moments beside the fire
  • A colorful voxel world
